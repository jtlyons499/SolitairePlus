# B86: poker rules audit and alignment

B86 (designer request) asked for every poker variant to follow one set of table rules, with deliberate
differences kept. The brief asked to stop and show Dan this table before the fix. Dan decided before the sprint
(2026-11-15) that B86 does not wait: unify the shared rules, keep the deliberate differences (Stud's bring-in,
Blind Man's Bluff's single card, Five-O's columns), and record the table for review. The original maintainer
added: "NPC behavior should be consistent between all variants, including holding grudges and behaving
differently based on the amount of cash on hand."

Sections: 1. the audit (how each variant behaved before B86); 2. the shared rules now in force; 3. what stays
different on purpose; 4. choices for the designer. Phase 7's report in [PHASE_REPORTS.md](PHASE_REPORTS.md)
lists the visible changes and tests.

## 2. Shared rules now in force

Each rule is written once and every single-player variant calls it. Table rules, pots, betting and hand evaluation
are in `solitaireplus/games/poker/core.py`, NPC decision inputs in `npc_inputs.py`, grudge recording in
`npc/personality.py` (`PokerGrudgeMemory.record_showdown`) and screen timers in `table_timers.py`. Since Phase 11,
multiplayer Hold'em rooms play on the same engine, and Blind Man's Bluff rooms settle pots, raises and winners with
the same core rules; room Blind Man's Bluff NPCs still use their own simpler decisions (see `docs/POKER_RULE_CHANGES.md`,
"Online play"). The Phase 11 steps are in `docs/dev/PHASE11_PLAN.md`.

## 3. Kept different on purpose

- **Stud's antes, bring-in, fixed-limit betting (small/big street bets, completion, 3-raise cap) and first actor by best exposed hand.** This is the traditional Seven-Card Stud structure, and Dan named Stud's bring-in as deliberate. Shared rules (raise guard, side pots, odd chips, short forced bet) still apply on top.
- **Draw's draw phase (NPCs draw instantly; an all-in human still chooses discards; the muck is reshuffled when the deck runs out).** The draw is not a betting action, so an all-in seat still improves its hand. This is variant structure, not a betting rule.
- **Five-O column structure: heads-up, five columns with the 4th card hidden, one betting round, one pot-based bet size capped at the smaller stack, full ante required (otherwise the session ends), 3-of-5 columns wins or the pot splits, column-by-column reveal.** Dan named Five-O's column structure. The capped bet size means Five-O never has all-ins or side pots, so a short-ante or rebuy rule would need machinery the game deliberately avoids.
- **Blind Man's Bluff single card: highest rank wins, suits never break ties, one betting round, every card visible except your own.** Dan named BMB's single card. Its evaluator is deliberately the rank of one card.
- **Per-variant stakes and wealth scaling factors (Hold'em/Draw blinds at 1% of buy-in, Stud ante/bring-in fractions, Five-O 1% ante and pot-based bet, BMB 0.8% ante; Draw scales wealth by 0.80 before the draw, Stud by 0.45-0.85 by street).** This is tuning for each table's structure and the information it reveals. Only the formula and signal are shared; the scale stays per variant.
- **Position labels: blinds, button and cutoff in Hold'em and Draw; 'unknown' in Stud, Five-O and BMB.** Stud acts by exposed cards, and the niche games have no button, so there is no positional meaning.
- **Manual next hand and Return in Five-O and BMB, vs auto-deal and a 5 s session-end timer in Hold'em, Draw and Stud.** This is session UX rather than a poker rule. Changing it would alter each screen's visible flow without a rules benefit; revisit only if Dan wants identical session flow.
- **Multiplayer room features: buy-back, NPC replacement, sit-out, spectating host, auto-fold of absent humans, 6 s between hands.** Networked play needs these, and the single-player tables have no equivalent. Phase 11 moved multiplayer onto the shared engine without changing these features.
- **Showdown order and mucking: every live hand is shown in seat order, with no mucking.** This is already the same in every variant; no change is needed. Five-O's timed column reveal and BMB's always-visible cards are part of their structures.

## 4. Choices for the designer

These were decided by default during the sprint (Dan was away). Each is easy to change.

1. **Blind Man's Bluff short stacks** now play all-in on a short ante instead of sitting out, and the session only
   ends at 0 chips. This matches the other tables.
2. **Hold'em after you fold:** the NPCs play the hand out at the normal NPC and street delays, so at slow speed
   with 4 NPCs a preflop fold usually means about 30 s (up to about 50 s; about 20 s at normal speed) before
   Next Hand appears. Draw and Stud always worked this way.
   A spectator fast-forward would be a small addition.
3. **Stud short all-ins** never re-open betting and never count toward the 3-raise cap. TDA's limit rule
   (an all-in of at least half a bet re-opens) is not used.
4. **Stud bring-in when the lowest card is all-in (B40):** it goes to the next-lowest card that can post. The usual
   casino rule gives it to the player on the all-in seat's left; the two differ only with 4+ seats.
5. **Fold wins** are settled like a showdown (B86 polish P1): the winner's own uncalled bet is returned, not won
   ("Uncalled $980 returned to you. You win $50." after shoving 1,000 into blinds of 10 and 20; it used to say
   "You win $1,030"). Stacks end the same; the Hold'em pot achievements count only pots, and fans use the net.
6. **Win sound when you win a side pot but lose money on the hand:** you still get the win sound and highlight (you
   won a contested pot); only fans need a profit.
7. **The "who won" rule also applies to NPC seats** (highlight, the "shown weak" read, the hand history).
8. **"Shown weak" read:** in Hold'em, Draw and Stud it means a weak hand category (high card, one pair); in Five-O
   and BMB it means the seat won nothing.
9. **Five-O has no hand-win sound.**
10. **Five-O NPC bet and raise size** is the bet-size preset the human last picked (1/4 pot, 1/2 pot, pot, max). The
    presets are now locked while the NPC thinks, but the NPC still sizes its bets from your preset.
11. **Quick chat after you fold:** the lines that sway NPC decisions ("I've got a great hand", "I'm bored") are
    hidden; the social lines stay.
12. **Only Hold'em shows the Payout details recap.** Draw and Stud could show it too (their engines produce it).
13. **Seeded runs:** NPC talk in Five-O and BMB now uses the random number stream differently, so a seeded
    replay recorded before B86 deals differently after the first NPC chat.
